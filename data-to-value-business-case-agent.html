ABOUT THIS AGENT

What it does

Gives an account executive a defensible, sourced business case before the first call. Name a target company and a use case, and the agent researches the account, ranks the customer's most expensive problems, and produces a strategy blueprint plus an interactive app for discovery conversations.

How it works

1Give it a target company and an Alteryx One use case in a single prompt.
2It researches the account from live public sources: 10-Ks, investor days, earnings calls, and press releases.
3It ranks which team to call first by the estimated annual cost of the problem to the customer, not by Alteryx deal size.
4It maps business value to KPI, value driver, and Alteryx One capability, and builds an ROI model in conservative, base, and aggressive ranges.
5It returns a strategy blueprint and an interactive discovery app. The seller reviews both before anything reaches the customer.

SEMANTIC LAYER

The definitions behind the agent

What each term means, so every business case uses the same language. Defined once, these can be reused by other agents and by Slack-based tools.

Value chainBusiness Value, Success Metric, Value Driver, and Alteryx One capability. All four columns are always filled.
Value driversFour only: Revenue Growth, Risk Mitigation, Operating Margin Expansion, and Capital Efficiency.
Success metricAn explicit, trackable range that is cited or tagged illustrative, paired with an “Ask the prospect” question the seller can use verbatim.
Pain tiersTeams ranked by the estimated annual cost of the problem to the customer: Tier 1 Acute, Tier 2 Material, Tier 3 Emerging. Dollar thresholds are illustrative.
PersonasEach value driver maps to an executive sponsor, line-of-business champions, and the objections to expect.
Sales stagesContent maps to Alteryx's five sales stages.
DifferentiatorsThree to five chosen from a fixed library of eight, each tied to a specific customer pain.
