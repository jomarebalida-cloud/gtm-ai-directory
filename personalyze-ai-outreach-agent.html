Personalyze AI Outreach Agent

Turns Salesforce, Marketo, and LinkedIn data into personalized email drafts and LinkedIn messages inside Outreach tasks, so reps spend less time on research and send a higher volume of personalized outreach.

Open in Outreach Request a change
ABOUT THIS AGENT

What it does

Built and maintained internally by Data Science and integrated into the Outreach platform. Reps no longer check multiple tabs: generated messaging and prospect notes sit inside the task window, and reps can edit every draft. SDRs use it for about a third of their outreach today.

How it works

1A rep adds leads or contacts to an Outreach sequence with “Personalyze AI” in its name, labeled by role (outbound or inbound).
2Every 30 minutes, a batch process scans those sequences.
3It pulls prospect data and consolidates account and person-level information from Salesforce, Marketo, LinkedIn, and other sources.
4It generates 3 personalized emails and 1 LinkedIn message, plus a prospect summary and notes, and uploads them to Outreach.
5The rep reviews the drafts in the task window and edits them as needed.
